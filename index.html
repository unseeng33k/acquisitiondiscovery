Model rule: filter options by launch deadline and launch budget, then choose the lowest modeled 12-month cost. Strategic fit, retention and business outcomes need stakeholder validation.

Dependencies, not just dates

Click a workstream for its acceptance criteria and dependencies. Dark bars mark one critical path. Parallel work assumes separate teams; capacity and uncertainty are not modeled.

Inspect the model, scope and assumptions

No patient data or real company systems are used. Calendar-day task durations are illustrative. Launch investment = base cost + rounded-up schedule weeks × weekly team cost. Source quality adds up to 10 days each to profiling and reconciliation. Interface availability affects migration and bridge paths; retained operations do not require that interface. There is no probabilistic forecast, resource leveling or verified regulatory determination.

Out of scope: patient services, pharmacovigilance integration, safety-case migration and regulatory submissions. If required, those are additional workstreams that can change feasibility. Discovery must identify that scope before commitment.
